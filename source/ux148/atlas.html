Equations and sources

The fork

ψ(x, y) = (x + iy) · exp(iky)

Look at the dark point in the middle: one stripe ends there, so one side carries one stripe more than the other.

Nye JF, Berry MV · Proc R Soc Lond A 1974;336:165–190

The honeycomb

u(r) = cos(k₁·r) + cos(k₂·r) + cos(k₃·r), ∠(kₙ, kₙ₊₁) = 120°

The cells stay where they are and only pulse: this is a standing wave, not a travelling one.

Faraday M · Philos Trans R Soc Lond 1831;121:299–340

Order without repeats

u(r) = Σ cos(kₙ·r), n = 1 … 6, ∠(kₙ, kₙ₊₁) = 30°

Try to find a place where the pattern repeats exactly. There is none: twelvefold symmetry and exact repetition do not go together.

Edwards WS, Fauve S · Phys Rev E 1993;47:R788–R791

The bend

I(x, z) = Ai²(x − (z/2)²)

The bright line follows a parabola. Nothing pushes it sideways: it is where parts of the wave add up, and that place moves as the wave travels.

Berry MV, Balazs NL · Am J Phys 1979;47:264–267
Siviloglou GA et al. · Phys Rev Lett 2007;99:213901
